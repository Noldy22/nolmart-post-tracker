# NolMart Post Tracker 📱✨

A high-performance mobile Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to track and execute content across all four core **NolMart** & **NolMart Scents** social media calendars, prompts, and launch playbooks.

Hosted on GitHub Pages: **[https://noldy22.github.io/nolmart-post-tracker/](https://noldy22.github.io/nolmart-post-tracker/)**

---

## 🎯 What This App Solves
Instead of opening multiple heavy Google Docs or switching between word processors while scheduling or creating content on your phone:
1. **Access all posts in one unified, mobile-first interface.**
2. **100% Verbatim Accuracy**: Prompts, captions, Swahili voiceover cues, hooks, and guidelines are preserved exactly as written in the source documents.
3. **Posted vs. Unposted Tracking**: Easily mark posts as posted with 1 tap. Separate posted content from pending content with dedicated status tabs.
4. **1-Tap Clipboard Copy**: Copy ready-to-paste prompts into ChatGPT / Google Flow / Gemini and ready-to-paste captions directly into TikTok / Instagram without manual highlighting.
5. **Document Source Separation**: Filter effortlessly by any of the 4 source documents.

## 📲 How to Install to Your Phone (PWA)

### On iPhone (Safari):
1. Open the live link: `https://noldy22.github.io/nolmart-post-tracker/` in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up at the bottom).
3. Scroll down and select **"Add to Home Screen"** (`+`).
4. Tap **Add**. The app will now appear on your home screen with the luxury NolMart icon like a native app!

### On Android (Chrome):
1. Open the live link: `https://noldy22.github.io/nolmart-post-tracker/` in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **Install** banner at the top, or tap the three dots (`⋮`) in the top right.
3. Select **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.
4. The app installs to your app drawer and home screen.

## ⚙️ Features
- **1-Click Copy**: Fast copy buttons for prompts, carousel slides, and captions.
- **Offline Mode**: Works 100% offline via Service Worker.
- **Auto-Save**: Progress and timestamps persist automatically in your phone's storage.
- **Backup & Restore**: Export your progress as a `.json` backup file or import it on another device anytime.
- **Instant Search**: Search across hooks, scents, formulas, captions, and hashtags in under 10ms.
