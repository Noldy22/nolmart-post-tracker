Why this bio wins against competitors
* It says what you sell in the first three seconds: perfumes and car air fresheners, with the emojis doing the explaining.
* It gives one hard, checkable difference. "60% oil" is a concrete number a visitor can compare. Many popular Eau de Parfum bottles sit around 15 to 20 percent oil, so your number is easy to remember and easy to repeat.
* It names the place and the maker. "Hand-blended in Dar es Salaam" builds trust in a market where people fear online scams.
* It ends with one next step (DM to order), never two.
If you deliver outside Dar es Salaam, add a line such as "Delivery across Tanzania" once you are sure it is true.

2.4 Posting Times and Daily Routine
* Main post: 7:30 PM East Africa Time, when people are relaxed and ready to engage.
* Optional light post at 12:30 PM: reshare the post as a Story or post a poll sticker. Skip it on tired days.
* Launch burst: publish Days 1, 2 and 3 on the same day, a few hours apart, so new visitors see a profile with content instead of an empty page. From Day 4 onward, post one per day.
* Daily engagement (15 minutes): leave 10 genuine comments on local Dar es Salaam business, car, beauty and fragrance accounts. Reply to every comment on your own posts within the hour in the first week.
2.5 More Ways to Get Known With Zero Followers
* Use Dar es Salaam and product words in captions and on-screen text, because both apps work like search engines.
* Make every post worth sharing: a tip to save, a poll to answer, or a friend to tag.
* Run the giveaway in Week 4 (Days 22 to 29) to bring in followers quickly.
* Turn buyers into proof: after each early order, ask permission to share a screenshot or photo of the customer's message.
3. Production Guide for Gemini
3.1 Sizes
* Images, carousels and single graphics: 4:5 portrait, 1080 by 1350 pixels. This is the Instagram size, and it also works on TikTok photo posts.
* Videos: vertical 9:16, 1080 by 1920 pixels, always one 10-second shot.
* These sizes are already written inside every prompt. If Gemini gives you a different size, crop or resize in your phone gallery or Canva, and keep the important elements in the centre.
3.2 Making a Carousel Look Like One Set
1. In a new Gemini chat, paste the prompt for slide 1 (the cover) and regenerate until you love it.
2. In the same chat, attach slide 1, then paste the prompt for slide 2. Repeat for every slide.
3. Check each slide against slide 1. If the background colour, gold shade, bottle shape or text style drifts, regenerate that slide.
4. Start a new chat for every new carousel.
5. AI text can contain spelling errors. If a word is wrong, regenerate once, or remove the word from the prompt and add it yourself in Canva or the Instagram editor.
3.3 Video Rule: One 10-Second Shot Only
Every video in this playbook is a single 10-second shot made from one prompt. Continuing a video from the last frame of a previous clip did not work reliably, so that method has been removed completely. Each video prompt describes the whole story of its 10 seconds, from start to final pose, so the clip can be posted exactly as Gemini creates it.
* Use one prompt, generate once, and regenerate only if the result is wrong.
* Add the text overlays and sound in CapCut or the app editor, because text inside AI video is unreliable.
* If you want a longer video, post two different 10-second videos on two different days instead of joining them.
3.4 Planning Around Your Video Limit (About 2 Videos Every 6 Hours)
* Run one video session each Sunday with two windows. Window 1 (morning): the videos for Monday and Wednesday. Window 2 (about 6 hours later): the Friday video plus one spare attempt for any retry.
* That gives you 4 attempts for 3 videos a week, so one failed generation never ruins the week.
* Carousels and single images do not use the video limit, so generate those during the week.
3.5 Fixing Common AI Problems
Problem
	What to do
	Misspelled text
	Regenerate once. If it is still wrong, delete that word from the prompt and add it yourself in Canva or the Instagram editor.
	The bottle changes shape
	Attach the previous slide as a reference and add the sentence: "Keep the bottle exactly the same as in the attached image."
	A person or hand appears
	Add at the start and the end of the prompt: "No people, no faces and no hands."
	Text appears inside a video
	Add: "No text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere in the video," and remove the text in the editor.
	Colours drift
	Regenerate and keep the hex colour codes in the prompt.
	The video ends in a different place than planned
	Rewrite the ENDING line in one sentence describing the exact final pose.

5. Captions and Hashtags

5.1 Caption Formula (Every Post)

6. Claims to Avoid and Rules to Follow
* Never say "alcohol-free" or "no alcohol" about your perfumes. The formula is 60 percent oil and 40 percent ethanol, so say "60 percent oil." The car air fresheners contain no ethanol, so that claim is fine for them only.
* Do not use designer brand names, "original" or "authentic" in captions or on screen. Use your own scent names. Brand names can cause takedowns and look less trustworthy.
* Do not promise skin results such as "will not irritate your skin." Suggest a small test on the skin first.
* Car air freshener duration: say "several weeks, depending on heat" until your stability records confirm a number, and never say "never fades."
* Always include the safety line on car air freshener posts: not for drinking, keep away from children and pets, and keep off the dashboard, plastic and leather.
* Gel tin: do not advertise it until its own test is complete.
* Hot car test (Day 15): publish only what you measured, with the real number of weeks.
* Giveaway: free to enter with no purchase needed, a clear closing time, a line saying the platform is not involved, prizes delivered as promised, and a check of local promotion rules.
* Customer messages and photos: share only with permission.
* Prices: publish only confirmed prices. Offer wholesale pricing only to wholesale enquiries.
