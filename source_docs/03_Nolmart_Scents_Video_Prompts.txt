Video Generation Prompts for Google Flow
Thirty-one ready-to-paste prompts for 10 second advertisement videos, each followed by a caption that matches what the video shows. There are 8 videos for the hanging car air fresheners and 23 videos for the perfumes. Every prompt follows the simple studio format that worked best in your tests: one product, a plain background, no people, a few scent ingredients, and four short scenes with smooth transitions.
Prepared in October 2026 from the NolMart Perfume Standard Operating Procedures (version 3.1) and your feedback on the first two test videos.
1. How to Use This Document
1. Take one clear photo of your real product on a plain background: the hanging air freshener bottle for Section 2, or the perfume bottle for Sections 3 to 5. Shoot it straight on, in good light, with the label sharp and fully readable. For the hanging air freshener, make sure the cord is clearly visible in the photo, because the prompt tells Google Flow to copy the cord exactly from it.
2. Open Google Flow, choose the vertical 9:16 format and the longest clip length (10 seconds), and attach your product photo as the reference image. The names of these options may differ in your version of Flow.
3. Copy the full prompt from the purple box and paste it in. Generate. If you are on limited credits, test one prompt first and keep the best take.
4. Check every take for three things: the bottle looks identical in every scene, the cord (for car air fresheners) is one single strand, and no ingredient passes in front of the label. If something is wrong, add one of the fix sentences from Section 6 to the end of the prompt and generate again.
5. Add your own text in the Instagram editor or CapCut, because the video is generated without any words. Good places are the last two seconds, where the prompt leaves clean empty space in the upper third. For car air fresheners, add 10,000 Tzs and your call and WhatsApp number there.
6. Paste the green caption box as the caption of the post. Each caption follows your caption formula: a hook line, short body paragraphs that match the video, a soft call to action and exactly five hashtags, with #NolMart first and #NolMartScents included.
What we learned from your tests, and how these prompts use it:
* Complex scenes fail: people in seats, a bottle on a rear-view mirror and a closing door were too much for the generator. These prompts have no people, no car and no hands.
* Simple studio shots work: a bottle floating on a plain background, or standing on a flat surface, with ingredients moving around it. Every prompt uses one of those two styles.
* One small move per scene works best: each scene has one camera move and one ingredient movement, so nothing competes for attention.
* The cord problem: the first test showed an extra branch on the cord. Every car air freshener prompt now has a cord rule that forces one single strand copied from your photo, and the avoid list repeats it.
About the formulas and brand names: the flagship captions describe only the smell and mood, never the ingredient ratios. Some pure scents carry designer names on your labels. The prompts never use those names inside the video, so only the text printed on your own bottle appears. The captions use your scent names as written in your Standard Operating Procedures, so rename any of them if you prefer.
About prices in the captions: standard 10 millilitre perfumes are 10,000 Tzs, Tom Ford Noir Extreme starts at 12,000 Tzs, 212 VIP Men starts at 10,000 Tzs, Obsidian Reef starts at 13,000 Tzs, and the flagship scents start at 18,000 Tzs for the 10 millilitre bottle. These come from your procedures. Signature blends have no price in the caption, so customers message you for it. The hanging air freshener is 10,000 Tzs.

1. Vanilla 28 Car Freshener, Floating
Video style: The bottle floats in a plain studio background and the scent ingredients move around it.
How it smells: Warm, creamy and sweet, like vanilla cream and soft caramel.
Best used for: Long drives, traffic, evening commutes and a car that feels like a cozy lounge. Rated best choice in the Car Air Freshener Standard Operating Procedures.
Attach to Google Flow: Your photo of the hanging air freshener bottle.
Prompt to copy into Google Flow (copy everything inside the box):
FORMAT AND STYLE
A 10 second vertical video, 9:16 aspect ratio, 24 frames per second. A photorealistic, high-end studio commercial, as if filmed on a cinema camera with a macro lens and professional studio lighting. It has four scenes of about 2.5 seconds each, joined by smooth, soft transitions. The background is a pure, clean, seamless white studio backdrop, with no horizon line, no floor line and no shadows on the background. There are no people, no hands, no cars, no on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos and no watermarks anywhere in the video.
THE PRODUCT (use the attached reference image as the exact product)
The hero product is the hanging car air freshener shown in the attached image: a small glass bottle with a raw, natural wooden cap and a thin cord. Reproduce it exactly as it appears in the image: the same bottle shape and proportions, the same glass, the same liquid color, the same wooden cap with its natural grain, the same cord, and the same label and printing. Do not redesign, enlarge, recolor or invent anything on it, and do not add any text to it. The bottle floats weightlessly in mid-air, perfectly upright, with the wooden cap at the top and the label facing the camera. The cord floats just above the cap in one soft, gentle curve and drifts only slightly. CORD RULE: copy the cord exactly from the attached photo: the same single thin strand, the same thickness, the same color and the same way it is tied. The cord must stay one single strand in every scene. It must never split, branch, fork, tangle, double up, or grow extra strands, loops, knots or loose ends. The bottle never changes shape, size, color or label in any scene, and it is always the sharpest and most important object in the frame.
THE INGREDIENTS (use only these, nothing else)
Whole vanilla pods, white vanilla orchid flowers, one thick ribbon of creamy white cream, one thick ribbon of golden caramel, and tiny gold dust specks. The ingredients never touch, cover or hide the bottle's label.
LIGHTING AND COLOR
A large, soft studio light from the upper left, a thin bright rim light from behind that outlines the bottle, and soft reflections on the glass. The color palette is white, cream and honey gold. The mood is clean, premium, warm and sweet.
SCENE BY SCENE
[00:00 to 00:02.5] SCENE 1, THE REVEAL.
Shot: a wide shot slowly pushing in to a medium shot. Action: the bottle floats alone in the center of the empty space and turns very slightly left and then right, no more than about 15 degrees, so the label always stays readable. The cord drifts gently and stays one single strand. One bright streak of light sweeps across the glass from left to right. Sound: a soft, airy chime on the reveal. Transition: a soft light flash.
[00:02.5 to 00:05] SCENE 2, THE DETAIL.
Shot: a close macro shot of the wooden cap and the upper part of the bottle, with the camera sliding slowly from left to right and a very shallow depth of field. Action: The wooden grain, the glass and the liquid catch the light, and the liquid inside shimmers as the bottle sways gently. A few tiny golden dust specks drift through the blurred foreground. Sound: a quiet, warm shimmer. Transition: the tiny golden dust specks swell into a soft glowing blur that fills the frame, then clears.
[00:05 to 00:07.5] SCENE 3, THE INGREDIENTS.
Shot: a medium shot with the bottle in the center and the camera nearly still. Action: In slow motion, three vanilla pods, three white orchid flowers, the ribbon of cream and the ribbon of caramel glide in from outside the frame and travel around the bottle in a gentle, wide spiral, like a slow ring around a planet. The cream ribbon curves in a graceful S shape and the caramel ribbon curves the opposite way. The bottle stays still and sharp in the center, and nothing passes in front of its label. Sound: a soft rising shimmer with a gentle whoosh as the ingredients arrive. Transition: the ring of ingredients expands outward.
[00:07.5 to 00:10] SCENE 4, THE HERO HOLD.
Shot: the camera pulls back smoothly to a medium-wide shot. Action: The ingredients slow down and settle into a balanced, elegant arrangement hovering around the bottle: the vanilla pods crossed below it, an orchid on each side, and the cream and caramel ribbons forming a soft circle around it. Everything floats gently. The bottle sits in the lower middle of the frame, with clean empty space in the upper third for text to be added later. In the last half second the motion becomes almost still, with one small glint of light across the glass. Sound: the music resolves into one warm final note.
AUDIO OVERALL
A soft, warm, elegant instrumental with a gentle build, a soft whoosh on each transition and a gentle shimmer when the ingredients appear. No vocals and no spoken words.
AVOID
No text, subtitles, logos or brand names. No changes to the bottle's shape, color, cap, cord or label. No extra cord strands, branches, forks, knots, loops or loose ends. No people, hands, cars or other objects. No ingredients covering or touching the bottle. No cartoon or animated style. No sudden jumps, flickering or morphing of the bottle. No spoken dialogue.
