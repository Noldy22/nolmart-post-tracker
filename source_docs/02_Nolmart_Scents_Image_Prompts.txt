NolMart Scents
Image Generation Prompts for Instagram
Forty-three ready-to-paste prompts for ChatGPT image generation, in two parts. Part one (Sections 3 to 6) has 27 scent introduction prompts: 9 pure perfume scents, 10 signature blends, 4 flagship scents and 4 car air freshener scents. Each of those images shows how a scent smells and when it is best used, using only color, light and setting, with no words inside the picture. Part two (Section 7) has 16 creative sales post prompts: fun, attention-grabbing ideas with overlay text, built to get shares, comments and orders.
Prepared in October 2026 from the NolMart Perfume Standard Operating Procedures (version 3.1) and the NolMart Car Air Freshener Standard Operating Procedures (version 1.2).
1. How to Use This Document
1. Take one clear photo of your real bottle (or hanging bottle, or gel tin) on a plain background. Shoot it straight on, in good light, with the label sharp and fully readable. The same photo can be reused for every prompt of that product.
2. Open ChatGPT and start a new chat for every scent. A new chat stops the colors and props of the previous scent from leaking into the next one.
3. Attach your bottle photo to the chat, then copy the full prompt from the shaded box and paste it in. Send it.
4. Check the result. The bottle must look exactly like your photo, the label must be correct, and there must be no extra words. If something is wrong, use the follow-up prompts in Section 8.
5. Save the image and post it. For the scent introduction images (Sections 3 to 6), put the scent name, price and how to order in the caption and not in the image, so the picture itself explains the scent silently, like the page you took inspiration from. The creative sales posts in Section 7 are different: they carry short overlay text written inside each prompt.
About the picture size: every prompt asks for a 4:5 vertical image (1080 by 1350 pixels), which is the standard portrait size for an Instagram post and the same ratio used by the page in your screenshots. ChatGPT sometimes returns a slightly different pixel size. If that happens, crop or resize it to 1080 by 1350 pixels without stretching it. The prompts also keep the bottle in the middle so nothing important is cut off in the profile grid.
About the formulas: the flagship scents are private house blends. These prompts describe only the smell and the mood, never the ingredient ratios, so you can safely share the images and the prompts.
About the brand names: some of your pure scents are named after designer fragrances. The prompts never use those names, so ChatGPT will not try to draw other companies' logos. Only the text already printed on your own bottle will appear.
